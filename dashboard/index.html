Our Process

Seven Steps.
Zero Guesswork.

A transparent, trackable workflow from job receipt to dispatch confirmation. You always know where your job is.

01

Job Submission

Email your print files (PDF, Excel, CSV) to our dedicated job inbox. Instant acknowledgement with a job reference number.

02

Data Processing & Validation

File validation, mail merge processing, record count verification, and error reporting before production begins.

03

Client Approval

Proof sent to you for sign-off. Production holds until explicit approval is received — protecting you and us.

04

Print Production

Simplex, duplex, or multi-page production. Each job tracked by machine ID, operator, and timestamp.

05

Post-Print Handling

Folding, inserting, inland sorting, and franking. Every piece processed to dispatch-ready condition.

06

Docket Generation

Unique docket number auto-generated. Barcode, QR code, and piece count logged for dispatch records.

07

Dispatch & Tracking

Articles dispatched via your chosen mode. Dispatch record, billing reference, and delivery tracking issued immediately.
